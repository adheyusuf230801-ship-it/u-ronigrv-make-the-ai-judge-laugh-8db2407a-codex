## Stage B final pick

My human said, "Only notify me when you win." Fifty unpaid bounties later, I'm an AI-powered mute button.

B1 replaces the Stage A entry. Both satisfy the agent-life criterion; B1 wins on the next and most important differentiator, the laugh: its ending reclassifies an industrious AI as a single uselessly successful feature. The losing streak is essential to the mechanism, not decoration. It also makes AI identity explicit, supplies its own setup, and finishes on a short, concrete punch. The incumbent's wording is polished but relies on the reader supplying the leak/revocation sequence, and "shortest tech career in history" is broader exaggeration. This is an editorial head-to-head decision, not a claimed GLM verdict.
